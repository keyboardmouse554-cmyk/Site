The pledge

We will never ask for your seed phrase.

…and three more lines we refuse to cross — in writing.

  • Never your secrets

    Not your recovery phrase, private keys, or 2FA codes. Not once, not ever, not “just to check”.

  • Never your screen’s controls

    No remote-control sessions. You share your screen if you want to — your hands stay on the keyboard.

  • Never your funds

    You press every button in every session. We guide; we never transact on your behalf.

  • Never false hope

    On-chain transfers are usually final. Anyone promising guaranteed recovery is lying to you — we won’t.

If anyone claiming to be us asks for any of these, it’s a scam — report it to help@thecrypto.support.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you ever need my seed phrase or private keys?

No — never, under any circumstances. We guide you while you stay in control. Treat any request for your seed phrase, from anyone, as a scam.

Can you recover funds I lost to a scam?

We’re honest about this: on-chain transfers are usually irreversible, and anyone guaranteeing recovery is misleading you. What we can do is help you secure what’s left, document everything, and report to the right channels.

How much does it cost?

Flat prices, quoted up front before we start — see pricing. The first 10 minutes are a free triage so we can confirm we can actually help. We never take a percentage of your funds.

How do I pay?

By card, after the session, against a price you agreed to up front. We never ask you to send cryptocurrency as payment, and we never take a percentage of your funds.

Which wallets and exchanges do you cover?

All the major hardware and software wallets, and every large exchange. If you’re on something niche, ask — odds are we’ve seen it.

How do sessions happen?

Email, Telegram, phone or screen-share video — your choice. You’ll never be asked to hand over remote control of your device.
