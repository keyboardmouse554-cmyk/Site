Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

Only what you send us: your email address and the details of your support request. This website itself sets no tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics.

What we never ask for

Your seed phrase, private keys, passwords or 2FA codes. Do not include them in any message — we will delete messages containing them.

How we use it

To answer your request and provide the session you booked. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Retention

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to help you, then deleted on request.
