FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you ever need my seed phrase or private keys?

No — never, under any circumstances. We guide you while you stay in control. Treat any request for your seed phrase, from anyone, as a scam.

Can you recover funds I lost to a scam?

We're honest about this: on-chain transfers are usually irreversible, and anyone guaranteeing recovery is misleading you. What we can do is help you secure what's left, document everything, and report to the right channels.

Which wallets and exchanges do you cover?

All the major hardware and software wallets, and every large exchange. If you're on something niche, ask — odds are we've seen it.

How do sessions happen?

Chat, voice or screen-share video — your choice. You'll never be asked to hand over remote control of your device.
